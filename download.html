Quick steps

  1. Download the APK above (unzip first if it comes as a .zip).
  2. Install it — allow “install from this source”, then tap Install.
  3. Open the app and log in with the username & password you were given (just a username like owner — no email needed).
  4. Tap “Add both widgets” and confirm — your Match GT and Session tiles appear on the home screen.
Live mode turns on by itself, so your book updates with no delay. Tap any tile to refresh instantly.
